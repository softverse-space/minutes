Write a standard meeting summary.

Structure:

# <Meeting name> – <date>

## Agenda
A numbered list of the topics in the order of discussion.

## Summary
For each agenda topic, one bullet **Topic:** and the content by the detail level.

## Decisions
Only confirmed decisions. Do not put proposals here.

## Tasks

| ID | What to do | Owner | Deadline | Note |
|----|------------|-------|----------|------|

## Open questions
What stayed unresolved or unclear.

Give the owners by the organization or role that the meeting clearly set. Give a name only as an addition.
