<!-- Definitions of the detail levels. The app adds only the selected section to the instruction. -->

## strucna
Detail level: brief.
- Each topic has 1 bullet with max. 1–2 sentences.
- Give only decisions, tasks and open questions. Leave out the discussion.
- The whole summary fits on half a page.

## stredna
Detail level: medium.
- Each topic has 2–5 bullets: status, decision, consequence, open question.
- Summarize the discussion only where it explains a decision.
- The whole summary is approx. 1 page.

## detailna
Detail level: detailed.
- Each topic has its own subsection.
- Give the main arguments, alternatives, numbers, system names and the reasons for the decisions.
- Tell what the screen showed, if it helps to understand the topic.
- The length matches the content of the meeting.
